# Manuscript versions

- `Xia_Allan.docx` — IJHSR/ACS format, 10 pt, superscript citations after punctuation.
  This is the version archived on Zenodo (10.5281/zenodo.22243161).
- `nhsjs/` — NHSJS format, 12 pt, superscript citations before punctuation.
  Two files: anonymized standard-citation, and online-citation with full
  references inline in double parentheses.
- `research_paper.md` — plain-text version, citations as superscript numbers.

All three carry identical content and numbers; only formatting differs.
