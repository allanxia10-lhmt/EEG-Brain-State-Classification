# References

ACS style, numbered in order of first mention in the manuscript. Every entry
was checked against the publisher record for authors, title, journal, year,
volume, pages, and DOI.

**Verify these yourself before submitting anywhere.** Four entries (11, 14,
20, 24) use `et al.` because the author list is long; expand them from the
publisher page if a venue requires full lists. Two (16, 24) have no DOI:
Jasper 1958 pre-dates DOI assignment, and JMLR does not issue them.

---

### 1. Uber das Elektrenkephalogramm des Menschen

Berger, H. Uber das Elektrenkephalogramm des Menschen. *Arch. Psychiatr. Nervenkr.* **1929**, 87 (1), 527-570. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01797193

> The original description of the human alpha rhythm and its attenuation on eye opening. The finding this project confirms and then measures the cost of.

### 2. EEG Differences between Eyes-Closed and Eyes-Open Resting Conditions

Barry, R. J.; Clarke, A. R.; Johnstone, S. J.; Magee, C. A.; Rushby, J. A. EEG Differences between Eyes-Closed and Eyes-Open Resting Conditions. *Clin. Neurophysiol.* **2007**, 118 (12), 2765-2773. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.clinph.2007.07.028

> The systematic modern characterisation of the topography and band specificity of the contrast. Primary comparison for the replication check.

### 3. EEG Differences between Eyes-Closed and Eyes-Open Resting Remain in Healthy Ageing

Barry, R. J.; De Blasio, F. M. EEG Differences between Eyes-Closed and Eyes-Open Resting Remain in Healthy Ageing. *Biol. Psychol.* **2017**, 129, 293-304. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biopsycho.2017.09.010

> Shows the effect persists across the adult lifespan, supporting its use as a stable benchmark contrast.

### 4. Shaping Functional Architecture by Oscillatory Alpha Activity: Gating by Inhibition

Jensen, O.; Mazaheri, A. Shaping Functional Architecture by Oscillatory Alpha Activity: Gating by Inhibition. *Front. Hum. Neurosci.* **2010**, 4, 186. https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2010.00186

> The gating-by-inhibition account: alpha as active pulsed suppression rather than cortical idling.

### 5. Alpha-Band Oscillations, Attention, and Controlled Access to Stored Information

Klimesch, W. Alpha-Band Oscillations, Attention, and Controlled Access to Stored Information. *Trends Cogn. Sci.* **2012**, 16 (12), 606-617. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2012.10.007

> The inhibition-timing account, complementary to Jensen and Mazaheri.

### 6. Event-Related EEG/MEG Synchronization and Desynchronization: Basic Principles

Pfurtscheller, G.; Lopes da Silva, F. H. Event-Related EEG/MEG Synchronization and Desynchronization: Basic Principles. *Clin. Neurophysiol.* **1999**, 110 (11), 1842-1857. https://doi.org/10.1016/S1388-2457(99)00141-8

> Desynchronization following sensory input, which is what eye opening produces.

### 7. Thalamic Mechanisms of EEG Alpha Rhythms and Their Pathological Implications

Hughes, S. W.; Crunelli, V. Thalamic Mechanisms of EEG Alpha Rhythms and Their Pathological Implications. *Neuroscientist* **2005**, 11 (4), 357-372. https://doi.org/10.1177/1073858405277450

> Locates the alpha generator in thalamocortical loops. Basis for the posterior-topography interpretation.

### 8. A Review of Classification Algorithms for EEG-Based Brain-Computer Interfaces: A 10 Year Update

Lotte, F.; Bougrain, L.; Cichocki, A.; Clerc, M.; Congedo, M.; Rakotomamonjy, A.; Yger, F. A Review of Classification Algorithms for EEG-Based Brain-Computer Interfaces: A 10 Year Update. *J. Neural Eng.* **2018**, 15 (3), 031005. https://doi.org/10.1088/1741-2552/aab2f2

> Reports that simple linear classifiers remain competitive on EEG. Directly consistent with the model-comparison result here.

### 9. MOABB: Trustworthy Algorithm Benchmarking for BCIs

Jayaram, V.; Barachant, A. MOABB: Trustworthy Algorithm Benchmarking for BCIs. *J. Neural Eng.* **2018**, 15 (6), 066011. https://doi.org/10.1088/1741-2552/aadea0

> Argues EEG classification results are frequently not comparable across studies. Motivates measuring marginal value rather than reporting one accuracy.

### 10. EEG Motor Movement/Imagery Database (eegmmidb), version 1.0.0

Schalk, G.; McFarland, D. J.; Hinterberger, T.; Birbaumer, N.; Wolpaw, J. R. EEG Motor Movement/Imagery Database (eegmmidb), version 1.0.0. *PhysioNet* **2009**, dataset. https://doi.org/10.13026/C28G6P

> The dataset analysed. ODC-BY licensed.

### 11. PhysioBank, PhysioToolkit, and PhysioNet: Components of a New Research Resource for Complex Physiologic Signals

Goldberger, A. L.; Amaral, L. A. N.; Glass, L.; et al. PhysioBank, PhysioToolkit, and PhysioNet: Components of a New Research Resource for Complex Physiologic Signals. *Circulation* **2000**, 101 (23), e215-e220. https://doi.org/10.1161/01.CIR.101.23.e215

### 12. BCI2000: A General-Purpose Brain-Computer Interface (BCI) System

Schalk, G.; McFarland, D. J.; Hinterberger, T.; Birbaumer, N.; Wolpaw, J. R. BCI2000: A General-Purpose Brain-Computer Interface (BCI) System. *IEEE Trans. Biomed. Eng.* **2004**, 51 (6), 1034-1043. https://doi.org/10.1109/TBME.2004.827072

### 13. This software

### 14. MEG and EEG Data Analysis with MNE-Python

Gramfort, A.; Luessi, M.; Larson, E.; et al. MEG and EEG Data Analysis with MNE-Python. *Front. Neurosci.* **2013**, 7, 267. https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2013.00267

### 15. EEGLAB: An Open Source Toolbox for Analysis of Single-Trial EEG Dynamics Including Independent Component Analysis

Delorme, A.; Makeig, S. EEGLAB: An Open Source Toolbox for Analysis of Single-Trial EEG Dynamics Including Independent Component Analysis. *J. Neurosci. Methods* **2004**, 134 (1), 9-21. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jneumeth.2003.10.009

### 16. The Ten-Twenty Electrode System of the International Federation

> The electrode naming standard the channel-cleaning code maps onto. No DOI; pre-dates DOI assignment.

### 17. The PREP Pipeline: Standardized Preprocessing for Large-Scale EEG Analysis

Bigdely-Shamlo, N.; Mullen, T.; Kothe, C.; Su, K.-M.; Robbins, K. A. The PREP Pipeline: Standardized Preprocessing for Large-Scale EEG Analysis. *Front. Neuroinform.* **2015**, 9, 16. https://doi.org/10.3389/fninf.2015.00016

> Source of the correlation-based bad-channel criterion, which is what stops the detector flagging every frontal electrode.

### 18. A Practical Guide to the Selection of Independent Components of the Electroencephalogram for Artifact Correction

### 19. The Use of Fast Fourier Transform for the Estimation of Power Spectra: A Method Based on Time Averaging over Short, Modified Periodograms
